# A Balloon Ride — Sky Explorers

An interactive 40-minute English lesson for Connect Plus Grade 5, Unit 2, Lesson 7. Built for classroom projection and independent practice on phones or computers.

## Run

Open `index.html`, or serve this folder with any static web server. No build, packages, login, API key or backend is required. GitHub Pages can publish the root folder of `main`.

## Learning sequence (40 minutes)

| Stop | Minutes | Learning activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Get ready | 3 | Prediction, partner discussion and objectives |
| Word explorer | 6 | Story vocabulary, seven landscapes, textbook matching |
| Useful expressions | 4 | Ten expressions and role play |
| Story journey | 10 | Full story in six scenes, explanations and comprehension |
| Look closer | 4 | Descriptive language, discussion and balloon sequence |
| Flight challenge | 6 | Core selection of questions and event ordering |
| My balloon diary | 5 | Planning, tense support and a short diary |
| Happy landing | 2 | 3–2–1 exit ticket and reflection |

The challenge contains more practice than students need to finish during the six-minute class slot. Use 3 MCQs, 2 true/false questions and ordering in class; use the rest afterwards.

## Objectives and assessment

Students identify characters, setting, problem, solution and outcome; retell events; match the five textbook definitions; explain the Nile/ribbon comparison; discuss weather and personal preferences; plan and write a diary using past simple and present perfect.

30 practice points: 5 matching + 8 multiple choice + 6 true/false + 6 ordering positions + 5 missing words. Successful answers remain recorded as best practice. Completion stamps are self-marked participation markers. Open speaking/writing answers require teacher review. The writing checklist only detects a small set of word patterns and never claims to grade grammar or meaning.

## Sources and teaching content

Based on the two textbook-page photographs supplied by the user, titled **Unit 2, Lesson 7: Story: A Balloon Ride**. Printed story text is transcribed into six scenes. Handwritten annotations are not treated as authoritative answers. The printed vocabulary, comprehension, descriptive-language tip, discussion, planning and diary tasks are all represented.

English explanations, new practice questions, simple vocabulary definitions, model responses, a fictional sample diary and SVG artwork are teacher-created support. They are distinguishable from the story text. The original page photographs are not republished. Optional text-to-speech is a device-generated reading, not the textbook audio.

## Accessibility and privacy

Semantic controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, larger text, projector mode and keyboard/touch ordering buttons alongside mouse dragging. Diary drafts, settings, points and stamps stay in browser localStorage on the current device. No student data is uploaded. Download a diary as a text file. Optional Google Fonts may make a network request; system fonts provide a fallback. All other assets are local and the lesson works without third-party JavaScript.

The lesson timer can start/pause via its display; resets are separate. The Teacher desk includes the full plan, answer key and printable notes, including all six story scenes.

Teacher desk asks for the teacher password on every opening. It uses a browser-side classroom lock; the static repository and source content remain public. Closing the desk locks it again, and teacher notes are excluded from printing while locked.

## Files

`index.html`: semantic page shell. `style.css`: responsive styling. `app.js`: lesson content, SVG illustration, interactions and local persistence.
