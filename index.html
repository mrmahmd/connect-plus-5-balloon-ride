07 / MY BALLOON DIARY

You’re the storyteller.

Book exercises 5–6 · Imagine your own trip. Plan it, then write.

PAST SIMPLE

Finished events

subject + past verb

Yesterday, I flew over the desert.
The sun shone. I saw dunes.

went · saw · flew · was · felt · climbed
PRESENT PERFECT

Experience up to now

have / has + past participle

I have flown in a balloon.
I have never seen such a view.

go → gone · see → seen · fly → flown

Use yesterday / last… with the past simple. Use the present perfect for your experience without a finished past-time expression.

TEACHER-CREATED MODEL · AN IMAGINARY TRIP

Dear Diary,
Yesterday, I went on a hot air balloon ride. The weather was sunny with a gentle wind. I saw the desert and golden dunes. The houses looked tiny below us. I felt excited. I have never seen such a beautiful view!

Spot the tenses: went, was, saw, looked, felt = past simple; have never seen = present perfect.

A complete 40-minute lesson · Grade 5 · A Balloon Ride

Success criteria

  • Retell at least four events in order and explain the pilot’s invitation.
  • Match the five textbook vocabulary definitions.
  • Explain that “silver ribbon” describes how the Nile looks.
  • Discuss the weather and support a personal opinion with a reason.
  • Write a short diary with past events and a present-perfect experience.

Assessment: immediate feedback for closed questions; partner discussion and teacher review for open answers. Writing checks are prompts, not automatic grammar marking.

Differentiation: use sentence frames and paired reading for support; add a descriptive comparison for extension. Optional reading uses the device’s speech voice when available.

Answer key & content notes

The clean story text follows the printed text in the supplied photos. Handwritten notes are not used as an answer key. New practice and the sample diary are teaching material.
